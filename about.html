About

Designer, researcher, detail person.

I'm Sidharth, a product designer focused on building digital products that are simple to use and genuinely useful.

My work spans user research, interaction design, visual design and design systems. I enjoy the whole arc of a project — from framing the right problem to sweating the micro-interactions that make a product feel finished.

I believe the best design is a team sport. I like working side by side with engineers and product managers, sharing work early and letting evidence, not opinion, steer the decisions.

Outside of work you'll find me sketching, reading about architecture, or looking for the best coffee in town.
